Fishing in the Delaware River Region

Explore fishing in the Delaware River Region of New Jersey. Delaware River, Delaware Bay, Salem River, Cohansey River, M

The upper Delaware River forms the New Jersey-Pennsylvania border for 150 miles from the Water Gap to Trenton, offering world-class smallmouth bass and American shad fishing with diverse public access.

1. Delaware River (Upper)

Delaware River, Warren/Hunterdon counties

The tidal Delaware River from Trenton to the bay offers excellent striped bass and white perch fishing with urban access in Philadelphia-area waterfront parks.

Delaware Bay, Cumberland/Cape May counties

Fishing Tips for the Delaware River Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in New Jersey; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Delaware River Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
