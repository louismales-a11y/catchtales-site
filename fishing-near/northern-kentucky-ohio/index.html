Explore fishing in the Northern Kentucky & Ohio River Region of Kentucky. Ohio River Inlet, Licking River, Markland Dam,

The inlet streams feeding Ohio River offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Ohio River Inlet (Sauger, Catfish, Smallmouth Bass, Largemouth Bass, Crappie)

Ohio River Inlet, Kentucky

The inlet streams feeding Ohio River offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Walleye, sauger & hybrids · Limit: 6 (12 possession) · Min 14 in
  • Channel catfish 28 in+ · Limit: 1 (2 possession) · Min 28 in; no limit under 28 in
  • Smallmouth bass · Limit: 6 (12 possession) · Min 12 in

Fishing Tips for the Northern Kentucky & Ohio River Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Kentucky; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Northern Kentucky & Ohio River Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
