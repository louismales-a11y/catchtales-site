The inlet streams feeding Ross Barnett Reservoir offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Ross Barnett Reservoir Inlet (Largemouth Bass, Crappie, Catfish, Bluegill, Striped Bass)

Ross Barnett Reservoir Inlet, Mississippi

The inlet streams feeding Ross Barnett Reservoir offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Crappie (black & white combined) · Limit: 30 per day
  • Catfish (state fishing lakes only) · Limit: 10 per day · No creel limit on other waters; border waters with AL/TN: 1 catfish over 34 in/day
  • Bream (bluegill, redear, other sunfish combined) · Limit: 100 per day
  • Striped bass & hybrids · Limit: 6 per day · Min 15 in

Fishing Tips for the Central Mississippi & Jackson Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Mississippi; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Central Mississippi & Jackson Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
