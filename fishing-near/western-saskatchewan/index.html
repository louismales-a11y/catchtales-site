1. Jackfish Lake (Walleye, Northern Pike, Perch)

Jackfish Lake, Saskatchewan

Jackfish Lake offers excellent walleye, northern pike, and yellow perch fishing. The lake's stained water and weed beds provide good habitat. Jackfish Lake has a regional park with camping.

Fishing Tips for the Western Saskatchewan: Lloydminster, Meadow Lake & Area

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Saskatchewan; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Western Saskatchewan: Lloydminster, Meadow Lake & Area waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
