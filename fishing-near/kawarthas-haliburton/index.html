9. Lake Simcoe (Lake Trout, Northern Pike, Walleye, Whitefish, Yellow Perch)

Barrie area, Ontario

Lake Simcoe is one of Ontario's most popular and productive fishing lakes. The lake offers exceptional fishing for lake trout, whitefish, yellow perch, walleye, and northern pike. Lake Simcoe is particularly famous for its ice fishing -- the lake hosts thousands of ice huts each winter, making it one of the premier ice fishing destinations in North America.

Fishing Tips for the Kawarthas & Haliburton

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Ontario; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Kawarthas & Haliburton waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
