Fishing in North Slave & Barrenlands

Explore fishing in the North Slave & Barrenlands of Northwest Territories. Lac la Martre, Snare Lake, Snare River, Faber

Lac la Martre offers excellent lake trout and northern pike fishing in a large, deep lake northwest of Yellowknife. Lake trout in the 5-15 pound range are common. The lake is accessible by float plane with several lodges operating on its shores.

Fishing Tips for the North Slave & Barrenlands

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Northwest Territories; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many North Slave & Barrenlands waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
