34. Big Trout Lake (Lake Trout, Northern Pike)

Far northern Ontario, fly-in only

Big Trout Lake is a large, remote lake in the far north of Ontario, located in the Kitchenuhmaykoosib Inninuwug First Nation territory. The lake offers excellent northern pike and lake trout fishing in a pristine wilderness setting. The lake is fly-in only, accessible through the community of Kitchenuhmaykoosib (Big Trout Lake). The lake's remote location and minimal fishing pressure produce quality fish. Lake trout in the 5-15 pound range are common.

  • Lake Trout · Limit: S-2; not more than 1 greater than 56 cm, and C-1; no size limit · January 1 to September 30 Limits: S-2; not more than 1 greater than 56 cm, and C-1; no size limit
  • Northern Pike · Limit: S-4 and C-2; none between 70-90 cm, not more than 1 greater than 90 cm · open all year Limits: S-4 and C-2; none between 70-90 cm, not more than 1 greater than 90 cm

Boat Launch: Big Trout Lake →

Fishing Tips for the Far North Ontario

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Ontario; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Far North Ontario waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
