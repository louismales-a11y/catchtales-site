Explore fishing in the Southern Minnesota Region of Minnesota. Minnesota River, Rochester area lakes, Beaver Dam Lake, K

The inlet streams feeding Mississippi River bluffs offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Minnesota River (Walleye, Largemouth Bass, Channel Catfish, Northern Pike, Crappie)

Minnesota River, Minnesota

Minnesota River offers excellent fishing in the Southern Minnesota with good public access and scenic surroundings.

  • Walleye and sauger · Limit: 6 combined (only 1 walleye over 20") · May 9, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027
  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 6 combined · C&R Mar 1-May 22; May 23, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027
  • Channel catfish · Limit: 5 (only 1 over 24") · Continuous
  • Crappie · Limit: 10 combined · Continuous

Fishing Tips for the Southern Minnesota Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Minnesota; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Southern Minnesota Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
