Fishing in Northern Saskatchewan: Lac La Ronge & Churchill River

Explore fishing in the Northern Saskatchewan: Lac La Ronge & Churchill River of Saskatchewan. Lac La Ronge, Churchill Ri

Lac La Ronge is the fishing heart of northern Saskatchewan. This massive, clear shield lake offers exceptional fishing for walleye, northern pike, and lake trout. Walleye in the 2-5 pound range are abundant. Lake trout fishing is excellent in the deeper basins, with fish in the 5-15 pound range. The lake has numerous lodges, outfitters, and public access points. Lac La Ronge Provincial Park offers camping and boat launches.

1. Lac La Ronge (Walleye, Northern Pike, Lake Trout)

Lac La Ronge is the fishing heart of northern Saskatchewan. This massive, clear shield lake offers exceptional fishing for walleye, northern pike, and lake trout. Walleye in the 2-5 pound range are abundant. Lake trout fishing is excellent in the deeper basins, with fish in the 5-15 pound range. The lake has numerous lodges, outfitters, and public access points. Lac La Ronge Provincial Park offers camping and boat launches.

  • ⚠ This water: Within 1 km of the river mouth closed to angling all year
  • *Walleye · Limit: 4 · only one may exceed 55 cm
  • Northern pike · Limit: 5 · only one may exceed 75 cm
  • Lake trout · Limit: 3 · only one may exceed 65 cm

Boat Launch: East Launch →

2. Churchill River System (Walleye, Northern Pike, Lake Trout, Arctic Grayling)

Flows through northern Saskatchewan

The Churchill River system is one of Saskatchewan's premier fishing destinations. The river's current, rapids, and deep pools hold walleye, northern pike, lake trout, and Arctic grayling. The river is accessible from several points including Lac La Ronge and Ile-a-la-Crosse. The Churchill River is a popular route for multi-day canoe trips.

  • *Walleye · Limit: 4 · only one may exceed 55 cm
  • Northern pike · Limit: 5 · only one may exceed 75 cm
  • Lake trout · Limit: 3 · only one may exceed 65 cm
  • Arctic grayling · Limit: 2 · only one may exceed 35 cm

Boat Launch: Otter Lake Boat Launch →

Fishing Tips for the Northern Saskatchewan: Lac La Ronge & Churchill River

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Saskatchewan; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Northern Saskatchewan: Lac La Ronge & Churchill River waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
