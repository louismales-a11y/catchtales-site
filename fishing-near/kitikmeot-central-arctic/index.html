Fishing in Kitikmeot & Central Arctic

Explore fishing in the Kitikmeot & Central Arctic of Nunavut. Kiglavaq River, Cambridge Bay Area, Ekalluk River, Kuglukt

The Kiglavaq River offers excellent Arctic char fishing in a stunning tundra setting near Kugluktuk. The short, powerful run produces large, brilliantly coloured char averaging 5-10 pounds. Fly-in lodges provide access to this premier char fishery.

Fishing Tips for the Kitikmeot & Central Arctic

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Nunavut; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Kitikmeot & Central Arctic waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
