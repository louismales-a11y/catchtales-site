Fishing in the Lakes Region & Lake Winnipesaukee

Explore fishing in the Lakes Region & Lake Winnipesaukee of New Hampshire. Lake Winnipesaukee, Squam Lake, Winnisquam La

Lake Winnipesaukee is New Hampshire's largest lake at 44,586 acres with 258 islands and over 200 miles of shoreline, offering world-class lake trout and smallmouth bass fishing with full-service marinas at Weirs Beach, Meredith, Wolfeboro, and Alton Bay.

Fishing Tips for the Lakes Region & Lake Winnipesaukee

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in New Hampshire; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Lakes Region & Lake Winnipesaukee waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
