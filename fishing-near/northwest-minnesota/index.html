Lake of the Woods Inlet, Minnesota

The inlet streams feeding Lake of the Woods offer good fishing for the region's target species.

Fishing Tips for the Northwest Minnesota Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Minnesota; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Northwest Minnesota Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
