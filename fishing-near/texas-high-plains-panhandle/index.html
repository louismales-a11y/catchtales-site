5. Bois D'Arc Lake (Rainbow Trout, Brown Trout, Brook Trout)

Bois D'Arc Lake, Texas

Popular fishing lake in the High Plains & Panhandle region of Texas.

  • ⚠ This water: For largemouth bass, only those 16 inches in length or less may be retained. Bass 24 inches or greater in length may be temporarily retained in a live well or other aerated holding device and immediately weighed using personal scales. Bass weighing 13 pounds or more may be donated to the ShareLunker Program; otherwise, the fish must be immediately released in the lake where caught. Anglers wishing to donate their bass must immediately cease fishing and contact TPWD at (903) 681-0550. Anglers may not remove the bass from the immediate vicinity of the reservoir unless instructed to do so by TPWD staff. Bass not accepted by TPWD must be immediately released. For smallmouth bass (if present), minimum length is 14 inches. No minimum length limit for Alabama, Guadalupe, and spotted bass (if present). Daily bag limit for all black bass species is 5 in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length limit or daily bag limit for common carp.
  • ⚠ This water: For channel and blue catfish, their hybrids and subspecies, 25 (in any combination - only 10 can be 20 inches or greater in length). For flathead catfish, minimum length = 18 inches and daily bag limit = 5.
  • ⚠ This water: For white and black crappie, their hybrids and subspecies, minimum length limit = 10 inches. Daily bag limit = 25 in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length or daily bag limit on bluegill, redear, warmouth or other species of sunfish.
  • ⚠ This water: For rainbow and brown trout, their hybrids and subspecies, there is no minimum length and daily bag limit = 5 trout in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: For white bass, minimum length = 10 inches and daily bag is 25.
  • ⚠ This water: For striped and hybrid striped bass, minimum length limit = 18 inches and daily bag limit = 5 in any combination.
  • Rainbow & brown trout · Limit: 5 combined · No minimum

Boat Launch: Bois D'Arc Lake →

Fishing Tips for the High Plains & Panhandle

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Texas; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many High Plains & Panhandle waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
