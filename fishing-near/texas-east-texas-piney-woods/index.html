Lake Fork, Texas

Lake Fork offers excellent fishing in the East Texas & Piney Woods of Texas with good public access.

  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 5 combined (largemouth, smallmouth, Alabama, Guadalupe, spotted) · Min 14 in

2. Toledo Bend (Largemouth Bass, Crappie, Bluegill, Channel Catfish)

Toledo Bend, Texas

Toledo Bend offers excellent fishing in the East Texas & Piney Woods of Texas with good public access.

  • ⚠ This water: Fish consumption advisory in effect.
  • ⚠ This water: For largemouth and smallmouth bass, minimum length limit = 14 inches. No minimum length limit for Alabama, Guadalupe, and spotted bass (if present). Daily bag limit for all black bass species is 8 in any combination. Possession limit = 10.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length limit or daily bag limit for common carp.
  • ⚠ This water: For blue and channel catfish, no minimum length limit; daily bag and possession limit = 50 in any combination, of which no more than 5 may be 30 inches or greater in length.
  • ⚠ This water: For flathead catfish, minimum length limit = 18 inches; daily bag and possession limit = 10.
  • ⚠ This water: For black and white crappie there is no MLL and daily bag = 25 crappie in combination.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length or daily bag limit on bluegill, redear, warmouth or other species of sunfish.
  • ⚠ This water: For rainbow and brown trout, their hybrids and subspecies, there is no minimum length and daily bag limit = 5 trout in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: For white bass, no MLL and daily bag = 25.
  • ⚠ This water: For striped bass, no MLL, daily bag = 5, and only 2 striped bass, 30 inches or greater, may be retained each day.
  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 5 combined (largemouth, smallmouth, Alabama, Guadalupe, spotted) · Min 14 in
  • Crappie (white, black) · Limit: 25 combined · Min 10 in
  • Sunfish (bluegill, redear, green, warmouth, longear) · Limit: No limit

Boat Launch: East Launch →

Popular fishing lake in the East Texas & Piney Woods region of Texas.

  • ⚠ This water: For largemouth and smallmouth bass, minimum length limit = 14 inches. No minimum length limit for Alabama, Guadalupe, and spotted bass. Daily bag limit for all black bass species is 5 in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length limit or daily bag limit for common carp.
  • ⚠ This water: For channel and blue catfish, their hybrids and subspecies, 25 (in any combination - only 10 can be 20 inches or greater in length). For flathead catfish, minimum length = 18 inches and daily bag limit = 5.
  • ⚠ This water: For black and white crappie caught from December 1 through the last day of February, there is no minimum length limit and all crappie caught must be retained. From March through November, minimum length is 10 inches. Year-round daily bag limit is 25 crappie in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: There is no minimum length or daily bag limit on bluegill, redear, warmouth or other species of sunfish.
  • ⚠ This water: For rainbow and brown trout, their hybrids and subspecies, there is no minimum length and daily bag limit = 5 trout in any combination.
  • ⚠ This water: For white bass, minimum length = 10 inches and daily bag is 25.
  • ⚠ This water: For striped and hybrid striped bass, minimum length limit = 18 inches and daily bag limit = 5 in any combination.
  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 5 combined (largemouth, smallmouth, Alabama, Guadalupe, spotted) · Min 14 in

Fishing Tips for the East Texas & Piney Woods

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Texas; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many East Texas & Piney Woods waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
