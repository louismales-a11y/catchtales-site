Fishing in Northern Manitoba: Flin Flon, Thompson & Clearwater Lake

Explore fishing in the Northern Manitoba: Flin Flon, Thompson & Clearwater Lake of Manitoba. Athapapuskow Lake, Phantom

Northern Manitoba is where the Canadian Shield dominates the landscape, creating thousands of deep, clear lakes that produce some of the finest fishing in the province. This region demands more travel to reach, but rewards anglers with trophy lake trout, northern pike, walleye, and smallmouth bass in a stunning wilderness setting.

1. Athapapuskow Lake (Lake Trout, Smallmouth Bass, Walleye)

Athapapuskow Lake, Manitoba

Athapapuskow Lake is world-renowned for its lake trout and smallmouth bass fishing. The lake's clear, rocky waters produce exceptional fish of both species. Lake trout in the 5-15 pound range are common. Smallmouth bass fishing is outstanding, with fish in the 2-4 pound range and a strong population. The lake also holds walleye and northern pike. Athapapuskow has several lodges and outfitters, making it a popular fly-in and drive-to destination.

  • Lake Trout · Limit: 1 · None may exceed 65 cm
  • Smallmouth Bass · Limit: 4 · None may exceed 45 cm
  • Walleye (Pickerel) & Sauger (either or combined) · Limit: 4 · None may exceed 55 cm

Boat Launch: Bakers Narrows Provincial Park →

2. Phantom Lake (Walleye, Northern Pike, Lake Trout)

Phantom Lake, Manitoba

Phantom Lake, adjacent to Flin Flon, offers excellent fishing for walleye, northern pike, and lake trout. The lake is easily accessible from the town and has a boat launch. Phantom Lake provides good fishing without requiring a long boat ride. Walleye in the 2-4 pound range are common. The lake is a popular destination for local anglers and visitors alike.

43. Clearwater Lake (Northern Pike, Walleye, Lake Trout, Smallmouth Bass)

Clearwater Lake, Manitoba

Clearwater Lake is one of the premier lake trout lakes in Manitoba, if not all of Canada. The lake sits in a meteorite impact crater and reaches depths of over 100 feet. The water is exceptionally clear, creating ideal conditions for lake trout that average 5-15 pounds, with trophy fish over 30 pounds caught regularly. Trolling with downriggers and large spoons is the standard technique. The lake also holds northern pike and walleye. Clearwater Lake has a provincial park with a campground, boat launch, and interpretive programs.

  • Northern Pike (Jackfish) · Limit: 4 · None may exceed 75 cm
  • Walleye (Pickerel) & Sauger (either or combined) · Limit: 4 · None may exceed 55 cm
  • Lake Trout · Limit: 1 · None may exceed 65 cm
  • Smallmouth Bass · Limit: 4 · None may exceed 45 cm

Fishing Tips for the Northern Manitoba: Flin Flon, Thompson & Clearwater Lake

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Manitoba; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Northern Manitoba: Flin Flon, Thompson & Clearwater Lake waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
