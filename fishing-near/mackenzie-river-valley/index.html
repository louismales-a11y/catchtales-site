Fishing in the Mackenzie River Valley

Explore fishing in the Mackenzie River Valley of Northwest Territories. Mackenzie River, South Nahanni River, Liard Rive

The Mackenzie River at Fort Providence offers inconnu (coney), Arctic grayling, and northern pike fishing. Inconnu can exceed 30 pounds and are powerful fighters. This is one of the best locations in Canada to target this unique species.

1. Mackenzie River (Arctic Grayling, Inconnu, Northern Pike)

Fort Providence area

The Mackenzie River at the confluence with the Liard River at Fort Simpson offers excellent inconnu, Arctic grayling, and northern pike fishing. The meeting of these two great rivers creates productive fishing conditions.

  • Arctic Grayling · Limit: DCL 3 / possession 5
  • Inconnu · Limit: DCL 5 / possession 10
  • Northern Pike · Limit: DCL 3 / possession 5

Boat Launch: Mackenzie River →

2. South Nahanni River (Arctic Grayling, Walleye)

Nahanni National Park

The South Nahanni River in Nahanni National Park offers Arctic grayling and walleye fishing in a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Grayling in the 14-18 inch range are common. The river's spectacular scenery includes Virginia Falls, twice the height of Niagara.

  • Arctic Grayling · Limit: DCL 3 / possession 5
  • Walleye · Limit: DCL 5 / possession 10

Boat Launch: South Nahanni River →

Fishing Tips for the Mackenzie River Valley

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Northwest Territories; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Mackenzie River Valley waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
