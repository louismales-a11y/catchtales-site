Fishing in Mackenzie Delta & Arctic Coast

Explore fishing in the Mackenzie Delta & Arctic Coast of Northwest Territories. Mackenzie River, Tree River, Coppermine

The Mackenzie Delta offers unique fishing for inconnu, Arctic grayling, and northern pike in a vast maze of channels and lakes. The delta's productive waters support healthy fish populations in a unique Arctic wetland environment.

Mackenzie Delta, Northwest Territories

The Mackenzie Delta offers unique fishing for inconnu, Arctic grayling, and northern pike in a vast maze of channels and lakes. The delta's productive waters support healthy fish populations in a unique Arctic wetland environment.

2. Tree River (Arctic Char)

Tree River area, Northwest Territories

The Tree River is one of the most famous Arctic char rivers in Canada. It produces large, brilliantly coloured Arctic char averaging 5-10 pounds in a short, powerful run. Fly-in lodges provide access to this premier char fishery.

  • Arctic Char · Limit: DCL 4 / possession 7

Fishing Tips for the Mackenzie Delta & Arctic Coast

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Northwest Territories; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Mackenzie Delta & Arctic Coast waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
