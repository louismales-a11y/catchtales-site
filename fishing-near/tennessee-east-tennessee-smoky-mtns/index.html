Explore fishing in the East Tennessee & Smoky Mtns of Tennessee. Great Smoky Mountains, Little River, South Holston Lake

Great Smoky Mountains offers excellent fishing in the East Tennessee & Smoky Mtns of Tennessee with good public access.

1. Great Smoky Mountains (Largemouth Bass, Crappie, Bluegill, Channel Catfish)

Great Smoky Mountains, Tennessee

Great Smoky Mountains offers excellent fishing in the East Tennessee & Smoky Mtns of Tennessee with good public access.

  • Crappie (all species) · Limit: 15 per day · Min 10 in (Region 1: 30, no size)
  • Yellow bass, bluegill, warmouth, bream, bullheads, pickerel, pike, yellow perch, nongame · Limit: No limit
  • Catfish · Limit: No limit under 34 in; only 1 over 34 in per day

Fishing Tips for the East Tennessee & Smoky Mtns

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Tennessee; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many East Tennessee & Smoky Mtns waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
