Fishing in the Eastern WA & Spokane

Explore fishing in the Eastern WA & Spokane of Washington. Spokane River, Lake Roosevelt, Pend Oreille River, Howard Lak

Spokane River offers excellent fishing in the Eastern WA & Spokane of Washington with good public access.

1. Spokane River (Channel Catfish, Smallmouth Bass, Largemouth Bass, Crappie)

Spokane River, Washington

Spokane River offers excellent fishing in the Eastern WA & Spokane of Washington with good public access.

  • ⚠ This water: [from Nine Mile Dam upstream to the Idaho/Washington state line] All species Selective gear rules.
  • ⚠ This water: [from Nine Mile Dam upstream to the Idaho/Washington state line] Trout Sat. before Memorial Day-Mar. 15 No min. size. Daily limit 2. Release all trout other than hatchery rainbow trout.
  • ⚠ This water: [from Nine Mile Dam upstream to the Idaho/Washington state line] Other game fish Sat. before Memorial Day-Mar. 15 Catch-and-release.
  • Channel catfish · Limit: 5/day (lakes) · No minimum; no limit in rivers/streams
  • Smallmouth bass · Limit: 10/day (lakes); no limit in rivers/streams/beaver ponds · Only 1 over 14 in in lakes
  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 5/day (lakes); no limit in rivers/streams/beaver ponds · Only bass under 12 in, plus 1 over 17 in, in lakes
  • Crappie, perch, sunfish, suckers, pikeminnow, rock bass, striped bass, non-channel catfish · Limit: No daily limit · No minimum

Boat Launch: Nine Mile Boat Launch →

Fishing Tips for the Eastern WA & Spokane

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Washington; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Eastern WA & Spokane waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
