Fishing in Kluane & Southwest Yukon

Explore fishing in the Kluane & Southwest Yukon of Yukon. Kluane Lake, Kathleen River, Aishihik Lake, Dezadeash Lake, Pi

Kluane Lake is one of Yukon's premier fishing lakes. This large, deep, incredibly clear lake offers excellent lake trout in the 5-15 pound range and Arctic grayling in its tributaries. The lake is accessible from the Alaska Highway near Destruction Bay with stunning views of the Kluane icefields.

1. Kluane Lake (Arctic Grayling, Lake Trout)

Kluane Lake area, Alaska Highway

Kluane Lake is one of Yukon's premier fishing lakes. This large, deep, incredibly clear lake offers excellent lake trout in the 5-15 pound range and Arctic grayling in its tributaries. The lake is accessible from the Alaska Highway with stunning views of the Kluane icefields.

  • Arctic grayling · Limit: daily 5 / possession 10 · Only one may be longer than 40 cm
  • Lake trout · Limit: daily 3 / possession 6 · Only one may be longer than 65 cm

Fishing Tips for the Kluane & Southwest Yukon

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Yukon; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Kluane & Southwest Yukon waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
