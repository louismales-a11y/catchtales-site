Explore fishing in the Central Alabama Region of Alabama. Lake Martin Inlet, Logan Martin Lake, Neely Henry Lake, Coosa

The inlet streams feeding Lake Martin offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Lake Martin Inlet (Largemouth Bass, Spotted Bass, Crappie, Striped Bass)

Lake Martin Inlet, Alabama

The inlet streams feeding Lake Martin offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Crappie · Limit: 30 · Min 9 in (exemptions: small impoundments, Weiss, Bear Creek, Chattahoochee reciprocal)
  • Saltwater striped bass & hybrids · Limit: 15 (max 5 over 22 in) · Chattahoochee: 15 (only 2 over 22 in)

Fishing Tips for the Central Alabama Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Alabama; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Central Alabama Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
