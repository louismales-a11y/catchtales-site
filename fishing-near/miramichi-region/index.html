Fishing in the Miramichi Region

Explore fishing in the Miramichi Region of New Brunswick. Southwest Miramichi River, Northwest Miramichi River, Little S

The Southwest Miramichi is one of the world's premier Atlantic salmon rivers. Runs of bright salmon from June through October attract anglers from around the globe. The river also holds excellent brook trout and smallmouth bass.

1. Southwest Miramichi River (Atlantic Salmon, Brook Trout, Smallmouth Bass)

Miramichi area

The Southwest Miramichi is one of the world's premier Atlantic salmon rivers. Runs of bright salmon from June through October attract anglers from around the globe. The river also holds excellent brook trout and smallmouth bass.

Fishing Tips for the Miramichi Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in New Brunswick; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Miramichi Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
