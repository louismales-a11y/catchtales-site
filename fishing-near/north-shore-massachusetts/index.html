Fishing in the North Shore & Merrimack Region

Explore fishing in the North Shore & Merrimack Region of Massachusetts. Merrimack River Inlet, Plum Island, Ipswich Bay,

The inlet streams feeding Merrimack River offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Merrimack River Inlet (Striped Bass, Bluefish, Flounder, Rainbow Trout, Smallmouth Bass)

Merrimack River Inlet, Massachusetts

The inlet streams feeding Merrimack River offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Striped bass (freshwater/tidal) · Limit: 1 per day · 28-31 in slot (varies by season)
  • Bluefish · Limit: 5 fish (year round) · No minimum
  • Fluke (summer flounder) · Limit: 5 fish (May 24 - Sep 23) · 16.5 in (shore); 17.5 in (vessel)
  • Largemouth & smallmouth bass · Limit: 5 combined · Min 12 in in most waters

Fishing Tips for the North Shore & Merrimack Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Massachusetts; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many North Shore & Merrimack Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
