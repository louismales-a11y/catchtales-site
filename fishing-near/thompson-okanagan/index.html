Fishing in the Thompson & Okanagan

Explore fishing in the Thompson & Okanagan of British Columbia. Thompson River, Okanagan Lake, Kalamalka Lake, Skaha Lak

The Thompson River is one of the world's premier steelhead rivers. Both summer and winter steelhead runs provide world-class fishing in a dramatic canyon setting. Spey casting is the classic technique.

1. Thompson River (Steelhead)

Kamloops area

The Thompson River also offers excellent rainbow trout fishing, particularly in the spring and fall when trout feed on salmon eggs.

Fishing Tips for the Thompson & Okanagan

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in British Columbia; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Thompson & Okanagan waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
