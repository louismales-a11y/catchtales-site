Fishing in Great Bear Lake & Sahtu

Explore fishing in the Great Bear Lake & Sahtu of Northwest Territories. Great Bear Lake, Great Bear River, Deline, Norm

Great Bear Lake is widely considered the finest lake trout fishery in the world. The McTavish Arm offers exceptional trophy lake trout fishing, with fish over 50 pounds caught every year. The lake's pristine, incredibly clear waters allow sight fishing for these giants. Strictly catch-and-release.

1. Great Bear Lake (Lake Trout)

Great Bear Lake, McTavish Arm

The Keith Arm of Great Bear Lake offers excellent lake trout and Arctic grayling fishing. This arm is more accessible from the community of Deline and offers excellent fishing.

  • Lake Trout · Limit: DCL 3 / possession 5

Fishing Tips for the Great Bear Lake & Sahtu

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Northwest Territories; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Great Bear Lake & Sahtu waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
