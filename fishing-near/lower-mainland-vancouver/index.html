Fishing in the Lower Mainland & Vancouver

Explore fishing in the Lower Mainland & Vancouver of British Columbia. Fraser River, Howe Sound, Capilano Reservoir, Sey

The Fraser River is BC's mightiest salmon river, hosting all five Pacific salmon species plus white sturgeon. Fishing is excellent from the Vancouver harbour upstream to Hope for Chinook, coho, and pink salmon. Sturgeon fishing is world-class in the lower Fraser.

1. Fraser River (Vancouver to Hope)

Vancouver to Hope

The Fraser River through Richmond, New Westminster, and Mission hosts massive runs of Chinook, sockeye, coho, and pink salmon. Trolling and mooching are standard techniques.

Boat Launch: East Launch →

2. Howe Sound (Chinook Salmon)

Vancouver north shore

Howe Sound offers excellent saltwater fishing for Chinook salmon, coho, and halibut. Access from Horseshoe Bay, Porteau Cove, and Lions Bay. Trolling with downriggers is standard.

Boat Launch: Royal Vancouver Yacht Station →

Fishing Tips for the Lower Mainland & Vancouver

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in British Columbia; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Lower Mainland & Vancouver waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
