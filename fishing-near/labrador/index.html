Fishing in Labrador

Explore fishing in the Labrador of Newfoundland and Labrador. Churchill River, Churchill River, Eagle River, Pinware Riv

The Churchill River is one of the largest and most productive river systems in Canada. The upper Churchill offers exceptional brook trout, northern pike, and lake trout fishing in a vast wilderness watershed. Brook trout exceeding 5 pounds are possible. Fly-in lodges provide access.

Churchill Falls / Labrador City area

The Churchill River is one of the largest and most productive river systems in Canada. The upper Churchill offers exceptional brook trout, northern pike, and lake trout fishing in a vast wilderness watershed. Brook trout exceeding 5 pounds are possible. Fly-in lodges provide access.

3. Eagle River (Atlantic Salmon)

Eagle River, eastern Labrador

The Eagle River is one of the most productive Atlantic salmon rivers in North America. Remote and pristine, it produces exceptional salmon runs with fish in the 8-15 pound range and trophies over 25 pounds. Strictly catch-and-release, managed through licensed lodges. A true bucket-list destination.

Fishing Tips for the Labrador

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Newfoundland and Labrador; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Labrador waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
