Fishing in the Northern Delaware Region

Explore fishing in the Northern Delaware Region of Delaware. Brandywine Creek Inlet, White Clay Creek, Lums Pond, Newark

The inlet streams feeding Brandywine Creek offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Brandywine Creek Inlet (Largemouth Bass, Rainbow Trout, Smallmouth Bass, Crappie)

Brandywine Creek Inlet, Delaware

The inlet streams feeding Brandywine Creek offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 6 per day · Min 12 in (Becks Pond: 15 in, limit 2)
  • Trout · Limit: 6 per day (4 in fly-fishing-only waters) · No size limit; streams open 1st Sat Apr, ponds 1st Sat Mar
  • Smallmouth bass · Limit: 6 per day (no more than 1 over 17 in) · Slot 12-17 in must be released
  • Panfish (white perch, yellow perch, crappie, bluegill, pumpkinseed) · Limit: 50 per day (no more than 25 of one species) · No size limit

Boat Launch: Brandywine Creek Inlet →

Fishing Tips for the Northern Delaware Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Delaware; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Northern Delaware Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
