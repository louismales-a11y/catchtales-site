1. Great Slave Lake (Lake Trout)

East Arm area, east of Yellowknife

The East Arm of Great Slave Lake is famous for trophy lake trout fishing, with fish in the 15-30 pound range common. The deep, clear waters and rocky structure create ideal habitat for large lake trout. Fly-in lodges provide access.

Fishing Tips for the Yellowknife & East Arm

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Northwest Territories; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Yellowknife & East Arm waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
