Explore fishing in the North Central Minnesota Region of Minnesota. Leech Lake Inlet, Lake Winnibigoshish, Brainerd area

The inlet streams feeding Leech Lake offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Leech Lake Inlet (Walleye, Northern Pike, Muskellunge, Largemouth Bass, Crappie)

Leech Lake Inlet, Minnesota

The inlet streams feeding Leech Lake offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Walleye and sauger · Limit: 6 combined (only 1 walleye over 20") · May 9, 2026 - Feb 28, 2027

Fishing Tips for the North Central Minnesota Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Minnesota; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many North Central Minnesota Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
