Fishing in Cape Breton

Explore fishing in the Cape Breton of Nova Scotia. Margaree River, Bras d'Or Lakes, Clyburn River, Chéticamp River, Aspy

The Margaree River is one of Canada's most famous Atlantic salmon rivers. The river offers world-class salmon fishing with fish in the 8-15 pound range.

Fishing Tips for the Cape Breton

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Nova Scotia; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Cape Breton waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
