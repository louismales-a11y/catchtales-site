Fishing in Central Saskatchewan: Saskatoon & Area

Explore fishing in the Central Saskatchewan: Saskatoon & Area of Saskatchewan. South Saskatchewan River, Pike Lake, Blac

Central Saskatchewan, anchored by Saskatoon, offers a mix of prairie river fishing, parkland lakes, and the transition into the boreal forest. The South Saskatchewan River through Saskatoon provides excellent urban fishing, while a short drive in any direction leads to quality walleye, pike, and perch lakes.

1. South Saskatchewan River (Walleye, Northern Pike, Goldeye, Lake Sturgeon)

Through Saskatoon

The South Saskatchewan River through Saskatoon offers excellent urban fishing for walleye, northern pike, goldeye, and lake sturgeon. The walleye population has rebounded strongly in recent years. The river's current, deep pools, and structure hold fish throughout the summer. Key access points include the Meewasin Valley Trail, riverbank parks, and the weir area bouncing and jigging are productive techniques.

  • *Walleye · Limit: 4 · only one may exceed 55 cm
  • Northern pike · Limit: 5 · only one may exceed 75 cm
  • *Goldeye · Limit: 8
  • Sturgeon · Limit: 0

10. Quill Lakes (Northern Pike, Walleye, Perch)

The Quill Lakes — Big Quill, Little Quill, and Quill — are a unique chain of saline lakes that offer northern pike, walleye, and yellow perch fishing. Fishing Tips for the Central Saskatchewan: Saskatoon & Area

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Saskatchewan; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Central Saskatchewan: Saskatoon & Area waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
