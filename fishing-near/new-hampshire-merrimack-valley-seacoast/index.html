Fishing in the Merrimack Valley & Seacoast

Explore fishing in the Merrimack Valley & Seacoast of New Hampshire. Merrimack River, Great Bay, Hampton Beach/Seacoast,

The Merrimack River flows 107 miles through New Hampshire from Franklin to the Massachusetts border, offering excellent smallmouth bass and trout fishing with numerous public access points and the Merrimack River Greenway Trail.

Fishing Tips for the Merrimack Valley & Seacoast

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in New Hampshire; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Merrimack Valley & Seacoast waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
