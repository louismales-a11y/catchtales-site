Fishing in the Hampton Roads & Tidewater

Explore fishing in the Hampton Roads & Tidewater of Virginia. James River, York River, Back Bay, Little Annemessex River

James River offers excellent fishing in the Hampton Roads & Tidewater of Virginia with good public access.

1. James River (Channel Catfish, Smallmouth Bass, Largemouth Bass, Crappie)

James River, Virginia

James River offers excellent fishing in the Hampton Roads & Tidewater of Virginia with good public access.

  • Largemouth & smallmouth bass · Limit: 5 per day combined · No statewide length limits (lake exceptions apply)
  • Bluegill & other sunfish (excl. crappie, rock bass, Roanoke bass) · Limit: 50 per day combined · No statewide length limits

Boat Launch: Brown's Island / Haxal Canal Dock →

Fishing Tips for the Hampton Roads & Tidewater

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Virginia; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Hampton Roads & Tidewater waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
