Fishing in the Southern Lakes Region

Explore fishing in the Southern Lakes Region of Yukon. Teslin Lake, Watson Lake, Frances Lake, Liard River, Rancheria Ri

Teslin Lake straddles the Yukon-British Columbia border and offers excellent northern pike and lake trout fishing. Shallow, weedy bays hold pike in the 5-15 pound range, while deeper basins hold lake trout averaging 5-12 pounds. Accessible from the Alaska Highway near Teslin.

1. Teslin Lake (Lake Trout, Northern Pike)

Teslin area, Alaska Highway

Teslin Lake straddles the Yukon-British Columbia border and offers excellent northern pike and lake trout fishing. Shallow, weedy bays hold pike in the 5-15 pound range, while deeper basins hold lake trout averaging 5-12 pounds. Accessible from the Alaska Highway.

  • Lake trout · Limit: daily 3 / possession 6 · Only one may be longer than 65 cm
  • Northern pike · Limit: daily 5 / possession 10 · Only one may be longer than 75 cm

Fishing Tips for the Southern Lakes Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Yukon; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Southern Lakes Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
