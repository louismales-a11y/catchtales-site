Explore fishing in the Southeast Alaska Region of Alaska. Juneau Inlet, Ketchikan, Sitka, Inside Passage, Lake K10, New

The inlet streams feeding Juneau offer good fishing for the region's target species.

1. Juneau Inlet (Chinook Salmon, Coho Salmon, Halibut, Rainbow Trout)

Juneau Inlet, Alaska

The inlet streams feeding Juneau offer good fishing for the region's target species.

  • Coho salmon - freshwater · Limit: Yakutat: 4/day 16 in+ (8 possession); Southeast: 6/day 16 in+ (12 possession)
  • Halibut · Limit: Unguided: 2/day, 4 possession (Feb 1 - Dec 31; closed January) · No size limit; federal regs apply to charter/guided
  • Cutthroat & rainbow trout (combined) · Limit: 2/day (2 possession) · 11 in min, 22 in max

Fishing Tips for the Southeast Alaska Region

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Alaska; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Southeast Alaska Region waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
