Fishing in Vancouver Island

Explore fishing in the Vancouver Island of British Columbia. Campbell River, Port Alberni, Sooke, Victoria Harbour & Are

Campbell River is known as the "Salmon Capital of the World." The river offers excellent Chinook, coho, and pink salmon fishing. The Tyee Club has been based here since 1924.

Fishing Tips for the Vancouver Island

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in British Columbia; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Vancouver Island waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
