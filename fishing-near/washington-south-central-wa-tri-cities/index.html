1. Yakima River (Channel Catfish, Smallmouth Bass, Largemouth Bass, Crappie)

Yakima River, Washington

Yakima River offers excellent fishing in the South Central WA & Tri-Cities of Washington with good public access.

  • Channel catfish · Limit: 5/day (lakes) · No minimum; no limit in rivers/streams
  • Smallmouth bass · Limit: 10/day (lakes); no limit in rivers/streams/beaver ponds · Only 1 over 14 in in lakes
  • Largemouth bass · Limit: 5/day (lakes); no limit in rivers/streams/beaver ponds · Only bass under 12 in, plus 1 over 17 in, in lakes
  • Crappie, perch, sunfish, suckers, pikeminnow, rock bass, striped bass, non-channel catfish · Limit: No daily limit · No minimum

Fishing Tips for the South Central WA & Tri-Cities

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Washington; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many South Central WA & Tri-Cities waters fish best in spring and fall.
