Fishing in the White Mountains & North Country

Explore fishing in the White Mountains & North Country of New Hampshire. Connecticut River Headwaters, Lake Francis, Fir

The Connecticut River begins in the Connecticut Lakes region of northern Coos County, flowing 410 miles through New Hampshire as the state's western border and offering excellent trout and salmon fishing with numerous public access points from Pittsburg to Lebanon.

Fishing Tips for the White Mountains & North Country

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid state fishing licence — check your state's wildlife agency site.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in New Hampshire; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many White Mountains & North Country waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
