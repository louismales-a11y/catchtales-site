Explore fishing in the Fishing on the Great Northern Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador. Torrent River, St. Anthony

The Torrent River is one of Newfoundland's premier Atlantic salmon rivers, near St. Anthony on the Great Northern Peninsula. Known for large salmon in the 8-15 pound range, this relatively small river is ideal for wading and close-quarters fly fishing.

Fishing Tips for the Fishing on the Great Northern Peninsula

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Newfoundland and Labrador; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Fishing on the Great Northern Peninsula waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
