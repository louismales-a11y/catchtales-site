Fishing in Dawson & Klondike

Explore fishing in the Dawson & Klondike of Yukon. Yukon River, Klondike River, Bonanza Creek, Hunker Creek, Dominion Cr

The Yukon River at Dawson City offers exceptional Chinook salmon fishing. The river narrows near Dawson, concentrating migrating salmon. Chinook in the 30-50 pound range are common, with fish over 60 pounds possible. The Klondike River confluence creates productive holding water.

1. Yukon River (Chinook Salmon)

Dawson City area

The Yukon River at Dawson City offers exceptional Chinook salmon fishing. The river narrows concentrating migrating salmon. Chinook in the 30-50 pound range are common, with fish over 60 pounds possible. The Klondike River confluence creates productive holding water.

2. Klondike River (Arctic Grayling, Chinook Salmon)

Klondike River area, Yukon

The Klondike River offers Arctic grayling and occasional Chinook salmon fishing its confluence with the Yukon River. The river's clear, cold waters provide excellent grayling habitat. The legendary river of gold rush fame also holds good fishing.

Fishing Tips for the Dawson & Klondike

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Yukon; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Dawson & Klondike waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
