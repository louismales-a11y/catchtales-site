Fishing Tips for the Saguenay & Cote-Nord

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Quebec; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Saguenay & Cote-Nord waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
