30. Lake St. Joseph (Lake Trout, Northern Pike, Walleye)

Lake St. Joseph, Ontario

Lake St. Joseph is one of the larger lakes in northwestern Ontario, covering over 493 square kilometres. The lake offers excellent walleye, northern pike, and lake trout fishing in a remote shield setting. Lake St. Joseph has several lodges and is accessible by road from Sioux Lookout. The lake's clear waters and rocky structure provide excellent habitat for all three species.

  • Lake Trout · Limit: S-3 and C-1 · open all year Limits: S-3 and C-1
  • Northern Pike · Limit: S-6; not more than 2 greater than 61 cm, of which not more than 1 greater than 86 cm, and C-2; not more than 1 greater · open all year Limits: S-6; not more than 2 greater than 61 cm, of which not more than 1 greater
  • Walleye and Sauger combined · Limit: S-4 and C-2; not more than 1 greater than 46 cm · open all year Limits: S-4 and C-2; not more than 1 greater than 46 cm

Boat Launch: Lake St. Joseph →

Fishing Tips for the Lake of the Woods & Northwest Ontario

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Ontario; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Lake of the Woods & Northwest Ontario waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
