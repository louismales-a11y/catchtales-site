1. Soper River (Arctic Char)

Katannilik Park, Baffin Island

The Soper River in Katannilik Territorial Park offers spectacular Arctic char fishing in a unique Arctic oasis. The sheltered river valley supports lush vegetation. Char average 4-8 pounds. Accessible from Kimmirut or Iqaluit for multi-day canoe trips.

Fishing Tips for the Fishing on Baffin Island

  • Get a licence — All anglers need a valid fishing licence for your province — buy online before heading out.
  • Check the weather & water conditions — always check forecasts and wind before heading out, especially on big water.
  • Know the regulations — size and catch limits vary by water body in Nunavut; check the local regulations first.
  • Best times — early morning and late evening are prime. Many Fishing on Baffin Island waters fish best in spring and fall.
  • Use the right gear — match your rod, line, and tackle to the species you're chasing and the water you're fishing.
