Brag Board

Share catches, like, comment, and post bite reports on how the fishing went.

What it does

The Brag Board is CatchTales' community feed. Post your catch photos, like and comment on other anglers' brags, and show off your biggest fish.

It's also a fishing report board: post a bite report — how did the fishing go today? What spot, what conditions, how many fish? Text-first with an optional photo, so you can report even when the fish weren't biting.

How to use it

  1. Open the Brag Board tab.
  2. Tap the camera button to post a brag with a photo.
  3. Or tap the waves icon to post a text-first fishing report.
  4. Add a location, conditions, and catch count to your report.
  5. Like and comment on other anglers' posts.
  6. Tap a green species tag to open the full species profile.

The feed can be filtered: All / Brags / Reports, so you can focus on what you want to see.

Posting a bite report

Bite reports are text-first — perfect for sharing how the fishing went even without a photo. The app can auto-fill the current weather conditions for your report, and you pick the spot from your favorite spots list.

💡 Pro tips

Reports help other anglers! Share what spot you fished, what conditions you saw, and how many fish you caught — it builds a community picture of where the bite is on.
