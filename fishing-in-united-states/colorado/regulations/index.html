Colorado Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Some waters have more restrictive limits - check Special Regulations: Fishing Waters for the specific water.
  • ⚠ Fish on stringer/container or not returned to water immediately count toward the bag limit; released fish don't count.
  • ⚠ Transporting live fish without permits is prohibited.
  • ⚠ Illegal to take: greenback cutthroat trout, Colorado pikeminnow, razorback sucker, bonytail, humpback chub, roundtail chub, flannelmouth sucker, etc.
  • ⚠ Species not on the limits chart have no bag/possession limits or are restricted/illegal to take.
