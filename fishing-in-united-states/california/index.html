California has over 4,000 natural lakes and 1,500 major reservoirs. From Sierra Nevada trout and Shasta Lake bass to world-class saltwater fishing on the coast, California offers incredible fishing diversity.

Fishing Regulations

  • No more than one daily bag limit of each kind of fish may be taken or possessed unless otherwise authorized.
  • Steelhead Fishing Report and Restoration Card required when fishing for steelhead (rainbow trout >16 in in anadromous waters).
  • Winter period (Nov 16 - last Friday in April) on inland streams: zero-trout bag limit, only artificial lures with barbless hooks allowed for all species.
  • Hooks must be barbless in many special-regulation waters (check special regulations).
