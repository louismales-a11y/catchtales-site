Texas offers some of the most diverse fishing in America — from the Gulf Coast red snapper and speckled trout to the Hill Country spring-fed trout streams, and from the massive bass-fishing reservoirs of East Texas to the catfish rivers of the Panhandle. Texas has 3,300 miles of tidal shoreline, 7,000+ lakes, and 15 major river systems. Lake Fork is famous for trophy largemouth bass, and the Gulf provides world-class offshore fishing.

Fishing Regulations

  • A Texas fishing license with the appropriate endorsement is required (freshwater endorsement for inland waters, saltwater for coastal).
  • Game fish may be taken only by pole and line, except as otherwise provided in the guide.
  • Possession limits are twice the statewide daily bag limits.
  • For freshwater fishes not listed, there are no statewide daily bag or length limits.
  • Alligator gar harvested (except Falcon International Reservoir) must be reported within 24 hours via the Texas Hunt & Fish app.
