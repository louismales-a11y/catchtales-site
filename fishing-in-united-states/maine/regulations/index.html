Maine Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Two fishing zones: North Zone (Apr 1 - Sep 30 open water) and South Zone (Jan 1 - Dec 31 ice and open water).
  • ⚠ From Aug 16 - Sep 30 (North Zone): artificial lures/flies only, daily bag 1 landlocked salmon + 1 brook trout.
  • ⚠ Sea-run Atlantic salmon, redfin pickerel, swamp darter: endangered/threatened - no fishing permitted.
  • ⚠ Striped bass, shad, river herring, eel, sturgeon: see recreational angling for migratory fish.
