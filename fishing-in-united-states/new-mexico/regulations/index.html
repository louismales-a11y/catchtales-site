New Mexico Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Free Gila Trout Fishing Permit required for Black Canyon, Gilita, Mineral, Mogollon, Sapillo creeks, West Fork Gila River, Whitewater and Willow creeks.
  • ⚠ Trotlines limited to 25 hooks, one per angler, and only in listed river reaches/lakes.
  • ⚠ Possession limit is twice the daily bag limit for warmwater species.
  • ⚠ Any trout with red slash marks below the jaw is considered a cutthroat.

Seasons

Most warmwater species open year-round. Kokanee closed Oct 1 - Nov 10 at Heron Lake/Willow Creek. Winter trout waters stocked Nov 1 - Mar 31.

Licence Fees

NM fishing license required; Second Rod Validation for a second pole; fees per NMDOW.
