New Mexico offers surprisingly diverse fishing from the Rocky Mountain peaks of the north to the warm reservoirs of the south. The Rio Grande provides excellent brown and rainbow trout fishing, the Gila National Forest offers remote wilderness trout streams, and large reservoirs like Elephant Butte and Navajo Lake provide outstanding bass, walleye, and catfish fishing in a high-desert setting.
