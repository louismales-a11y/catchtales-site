Indiana has over 1,000 lakes — 1,466 named lakes per official catalogs — plus 45 miles of Lake Michigan shoreline and the Ohio River border. From Monroe Lake's bass to the Kankakee River's pike, the Hoosier State offers diverse fishing year-round.

Fishing Regulations

  • Possession limit is two times the daily bag limit (does not apply to fish processed and stored at primary residence).
  • Max 3 poles or hand lines at a time; each line max 3 hooks or lures.
  • Snagging is illegal from public waters; all paddlefish caught with sport gear must be released.
  • Endangered species (cisco, lake sturgeon, bantam sunfish, etc.) may not be taken.
  • Exotic species (bighead/silver carp, round goby, snakehead, etc.) must be killed immediately if caught.
  • Limb lines: max 10, tagged; trot lines: max 1 with 50 hooks.
