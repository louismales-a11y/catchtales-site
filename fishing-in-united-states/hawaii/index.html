Hawaii has only 5 natural lakes and 266 man-made reservoirs — it is defined by the Pacific Ocean. World-class offshore fishing for blue marlin, ahi tuna, mahi-mahi, and ono, plus unique shore fishing for bonefish, papio, and ulua across the islands.

Fishing Regulations

  • Freshwater fishing in Hawaii has minimal bag limits - freshwater species (tilapia, bass, peacock bass) are generally not regulated by bag limit in most waters.
  • Marine species have per-species limits in Marine Life Conservation Districts and regulated areas (check regulated areas per island).
  • No fishing in Hanauma Bay MLCD without permit; many MLCDs are no-take.
  • Coral and live rock may not be removed.
