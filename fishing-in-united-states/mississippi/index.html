Mississippi has over 1,400 lakes — including 6 major reservoirs, 22 managed public lakes, and unique oxbow lakes along the Mississippi River. From the Delta's big catfish to the Gulf Coast's speckled trout and redfish, the Magnolia State offers diverse fishing across six distinct regions. Some northern lakes are even stocked for ice fishing in winter.
