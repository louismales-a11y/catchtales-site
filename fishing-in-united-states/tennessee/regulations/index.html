Tennessee Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Several waters have exceptions to statewide limits - check Exceptions to Statewide Regulations by region.
  • ⚠ Possession limit is twice the daily creel limit unless otherwise stated.
  • ⚠ Anglers restricted to max 3 hooks per rod/pole/hand-held line (single, double, treble each count as 1); sabiki rigs exempt for shad/herring.
  • ⚠ Shovelnose sturgeon: no harvest allowed.
  • ⚠ Commercial fishing and invasive carp programs (TCHIP) in Tennessee/Cumberland rivers.
