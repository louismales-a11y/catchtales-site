Washington offers some of the best fishing in the Pacific Northwest. The Puget Sound provides salmon, halibut, and rockfish, the Olympic Peninsula features wild coastal steelhead streams, the Columbia River is famous for Chinook salmon and sturgeon, and Eastern Washington's lakes offer warmwater fishing for bass and walleye. The state has 8,000+ lakes and 70,000+ miles of rivers and streams.
