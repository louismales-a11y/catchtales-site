Virginia offers extraordinary fishing diversity from the Atlantic coast to the Blue Ridge Mountains. The Chesapeake Bay and Eastern Shore provide world-class striped bass, blue crab, and flounder fishing. The Shenandoah National Park features 100+ miles of brook trout streams, Smith Mountain Lake offers excellent bass and striper fishing, and the James River provides smallmouth bass and muskie fishing.

Fishing Regulations

  • Creel limits are per person per day.
  • It is unlawful to stock any species of fish into inland waters without DWR approval.
  • American shad: no possession (catch and release only).
  • Landowner permission required to fish on another's property (except designated stocked trout waters with signage).
