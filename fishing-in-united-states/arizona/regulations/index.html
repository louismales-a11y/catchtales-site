Arizona Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Statewide bag limits apply unless site-specific Special Regulations apply to that body of water.
  • ⚠ Possession limits are twice (2x) the daily bag limit for each species.
  • ⚠ Roundtail chub: catch and release only.
  • ⚠ Northern pike: unlimited, must be killed immediately or released.
  • ⚠ Community Fishing Program waters have reduced limits (catfish 2-4, trout 2-4, bass 1-2 @ 13 in min).
