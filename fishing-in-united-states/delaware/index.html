Delaware has 142 lakes and reservoirs plus 400 miles of tidal coastline along Delaware Bay and the Atlantic Ocean. Lums Pond and Trap Pond provide excellent largemouth bass and crappie fishing, while the Delaware River and Bay offer world-class striped bass, weakfish, and bluefish.
