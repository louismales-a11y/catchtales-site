Massachusetts has over 3,000 lakes and ponds — though only 533 are designated as "lakes." From the Berkshires' trout streams to Cape Cod's world-class striped bass fishing, the Bay State offers diverse fishing across six distinct regions.

Fishing Regulations

  • Trout catch-and-release season (artificial lures/flies only) applies in many stocked waters during late fall/winter.
  • Some waters have special regulations (artificial lures only, catch-and-release, size limits) - check the freshwater fishing guide by waterbody.
  • Fishing license required for ages 15+ (some exceptions).
  • Saltwater species (striped bass, black sea bass, tautog, scup, fluke, cod, haddock, etc.) have separate 2026 recreational limits in coastal/tidal waters - see the limits table (marked saltwater).
  • Striped bass gear restrictions, filleting requirements, and total-length measuring rules: see mass.gov/saltwater-regulations.
