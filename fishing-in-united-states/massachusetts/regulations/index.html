Massachusetts Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Trout catch-and-release season (artificial lures/flies only) applies in many stocked waters during late fall/winter.
  • ⚠ Some waters have special regulations (artificial lures only, catch-and-release, size limits) - check the freshwater fishing guide by waterbody.
  • ⚠ Fishing license required for ages 15+ (some exceptions).
  • ⚠ Saltwater species (striped bass, black sea bass, tautog, scup, fluke, cod, haddock, etc.) have separate 2026 recreational limits in coastal/tidal waters - see the limits table (marked saltwater).
  • ⚠ Striped bass gear restrictions, filleting requirements, and total-length measuring rules: see mass.gov/saltwater-regulations.

Waterbody-Specific Rules

Quabbin Reservoir
  • Trout: 5/day, 12 in minimum; lake trout 20 in minimum, 2/day; salmon 15 in minimum, 2/day.
Wachusett Reservoir
  • Trout: 5/day, 12 in minimum; salmon 15 in minimum.
Walden Pond
  • Trout: special regs per guide.
Upper & Lower Mystic Lakes
  • Trout: 5/day, 12 in minimum.
Chicopee River
  • Trout: artificial flies/lures only sections.
Swift River
  • Trout: special regs per guide.
Millers River
  • Trout: artificial lures only in sections.
Long Pond (Brewster)
  • Trout: special regs per guide.

Seasons

Most species open year-round; trout C&R season in late fall/winter on stocked waters.

Licence Fees

MA freshwater fishing license required (15+, resident $27.50 / non-resident $37.50); saltwater: MA recreational saltwater license required for most saltwater species (per DMF). Fees per MassWildlife/DMF.
