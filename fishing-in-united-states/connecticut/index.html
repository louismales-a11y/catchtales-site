Connecticut has over 3,000 lakes with 2,200+ larger than 10 acres and 180+ public fishing lakes, plus 618 miles of coastline on Long Island Sound. Candlewood Lake, the largest lake entirely within the state, offers excellent smallmouth and largemouth bass fishing, while the Connecticut River provides outstanding shad, striped bass, and northern pike fishing. Fishing Regulations

  • From March 1 until 6:00 am on the second Saturday of April, trout fishing is catch and release only statewide.
  • Taking of Atlantic salmon is prohibited (except stocked Naugatuck/Shetucket/Housatonic rivers and DEEP lakes per Commissioner's Declaration).
  • Spearing and bow-and-arrow prohibited in trout-stocked streams; spearing prohibited in all lakes and ponds.
  • Taking of elver eel, glass eel, and silver eel is prohibited.
  • Trout and Salmon Stamp may be required.
  • Bass management lakes have different regulations - check the lake/pond page for the specific waterbody.
