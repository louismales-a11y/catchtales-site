Ohio features world-class Lake Erie walleye and yellow perch fishing, plus excellent river and reservoir fishing across the state. Lake Erie is the walleye capital of the world with the Western Basin offering phenomenal spring fishing. The Ohio River provides smallmouth bass and catfish, Grand Lake St. Marys offers saugeye and crappie, and the inland reservoirs provide excellent largemouth bass and panfish fishing.
