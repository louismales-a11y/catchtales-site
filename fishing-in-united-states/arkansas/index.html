Arkansas has over 2,300 named lakes and reservoirs plus world-class trout fisheries on the White River. From Bull Shoals Lake to the Ouachitas and the Mississippi Delta, the Natural State offers incredible fishing diversity.

Fishing Regulations

  • Check the AGFC guidebook and Code of Regulations for waterbody-specific rules - many lakes/rivers have special limits.
  • Trout: most waters 5/day, 16-in minimum; tailwaters below dams have special limits (see waterbody_rules).
