Nevada offers surprising fishing diversity across 200+ lakes, reservoirs, and streams. The crystal-clear waters of Lake Tahoe, the ancient desert oasis of Pyramid Lake -- home to the prehistoric Lahontan cutthroat trout -- the mighty Colorado River forming the southern border, and high-desert reservoirs like Rye Patch and Wild Horse provide exceptional fishing for trout, bass, wipers, catfish, and crappie across five distinct regions.
