Minnesota is the Land of 10,000 Lakes — actually 11,842 lakes over 10 acres. With the headwaters of the Mississippi River, the north shore of Lake Superior, the Boundary Waters Canoe Area, and more lakeshore than California, Florida, and Hawaii combined, Minnesota offers world-class fishing for walleye, northern pike, muskellunge, smallmouth bass, lake trout, and panfish across six distinct regions.

Fishing Regulations

  • No province-wide barbless rule. Barbless required on specific trout streams with special regulations (e.g., Lawndale Creek).
  • Special regulations for individual lakes take precedence over statewide limits - check the special regulations list.
