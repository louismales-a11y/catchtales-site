Illinois has over 2,900 lakes (the vast majority man-made) and more than 84,000 ponds. Lake Michigan provides world-class salmon and trout fishing along 63 miles of shoreline. From the Chain O'Lakes to Carlyle Lake, the Prairie State offers incredible fishing diversity.

Fishing Regulations

  • Statewide limits apply except where site-specific regulations are more restrictive (posted at boat launches).
  • Endangered and threatened species may not be taken.
  • Sport fishing devices limited to 2 untagged devices under immediate control; trotlines max 50 hooks, checked every 24 hrs, tagged with name/address.
  • Transporting live aquatic life between waters is prohibited; AIS prevention (drain all water).
  • Snagging seasons: Sep 15 - Dec 15 and Mar 15 - May 15 below locks/dams on Illinois and Mississippi rivers.
