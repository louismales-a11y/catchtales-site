South Carolina offers outstanding fishing from the Blue Ridge Mountains to the Atlantic coast. The Santee Cooper Lakes (Lake Marion and Lake Moultrie) are famous for giant catfish and crappie, the Lowcountry offers exceptional inshore saltwater fishing for red drum and speckled trout, and the mountain trout streams of the Upstate provide excellent coldwater fishing. Charleston is one of the best fishing destinations on the East Coast.
