New Hampshire Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Many waters have special regulations (artificial lures only, catch-and-release, size/bag changes) - check the digest by waterbody.
  • ⚠ Saltwater species have separate rules.
  • ⚠ Landlocked salmon and brook trout have special slot limits on many waters.
