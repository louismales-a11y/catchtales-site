New Hampshire offers exceptional fishing across the Lakes Region, White Mountains, and 18 miles of Atlantic coastline. Lake Winnipesaukee — the state's largest lake at 44,586 acres — provides world-class lake trout and smallmouth bass fishing, the Connecticut and Merrimack Rivers offer outstanding fly fishing, the White Mountains feature hundreds of alpine trout ponds, and the seacoast provides striped bass and bluefish fishing in the Gulf of Maine.

Fishing Regulations

  • Many waters have special regulations (artificial lures only, catch-and-release, size/bag changes) - check the digest by waterbody.
  • Saltwater species have separate rules.
  • Landlocked salmon and brook trout have special slot limits on many waters.
