Montana Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ 3 fishing districts: Western (p23), Central (p50), Eastern (p79). District standard limits apply unless the water is listed in District Exceptions.
  • ⚠ Anglers moving between waters may possess only the limit for the water they are currently fishing.
  • ⚠ Noodling (catching fish by hand) is unlawful in all districts.
  • ⚠ Bull trout: all waters closed to angling; all fish must be released. Pallid sturgeon: endangered, release immediately.
  • ⚠ For species not listed, no limits on number taken or possessed.

Waterbody-Specific Rules

Red Rock Creek (Beaverhead drainage)
  • Closed year-round from Elk Lake Road to Upper Red Rock Lake; upstream open to artificial lures only, closed May 1 - Jun 14.

Seasons

Varies by district and water; check District Exceptions for specific waters.

Licence Fees

Montana fishing license required (resident/non-resident); Conservation License + fishing license. Paddlefish tag required.
