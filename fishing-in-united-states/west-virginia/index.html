West Virginia offers outstanding mountain fishing in the Appalachian highlands. The state has over 20,000 miles of streams including famous trout waters like the Elk River, Greenbrier River, and Cranberry River. The New River and Gauley River provide excellent smallmouth bass and muskie fishing, and large impoundments like Summersville Lake and Stonewall Jackson Lake offer walleye, bass, and crappie.
