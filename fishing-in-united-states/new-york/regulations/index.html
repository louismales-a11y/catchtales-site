New York Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ New York has 9 fishing regions; statewide limits apply unless the specific water is listed in the Special Regulations by county/water.
  • ⚠ Trout: many waters have special regulations (artificial lures only, catch-and-release, reduced limits) - check the special regs list.
  • ⚠ Muskellunge: min size varies by water (30-54 in).
  • ⚠ Possession limit is the same as the daily limit unless noted.
