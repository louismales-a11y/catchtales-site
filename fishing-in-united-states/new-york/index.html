New York offers world-class fishing across its diverse landscape. The Adirondacks feature 3,000+ lakes and ponds including Lake Champlain and the Saranac Lakes, the Finger Lakes offer exceptional trout and landlocked salmon fishing, the Catskills are famous for trout streams like the Beaverkill and Esopus, and the Great Lakes and Atlantic coast provide salmon, steelhead, striped bass, and fluke fishing.
