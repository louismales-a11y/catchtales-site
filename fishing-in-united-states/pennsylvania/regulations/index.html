Pennsylvania Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Regulatory periods based on calendar day; trout season opens 8 a.m.
  • ⚠ It is unlawful to cast repeatedly into a clearly visible bass spawning nest or redd.
  • ⚠ Mussels/clams and paddlefish/spotted gar: closed year-round.
  • ⚠ Trout permit required to fish stocked trout waters (rivers/streams); special regulation areas have their own programs.
