Pennsylvania Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Regulatory periods based on calendar day; trout season opens 8 a.m.
  • ⚠ It is unlawful to cast repeatedly into a clearly visible bass spawning nest or redd.
  • ⚠ Mussels/clams and paddlefish/spotted gar: closed year-round.
  • ⚠ Trout permit required to fish stocked trout waters (rivers/streams); special regulation areas have their own programs.

Waterbody-Specific Rules

Susquehanna River and tributaries
  • American shad, river herring, hickory shad: closed year-round.
Hopewell Township Park Lake
  • Bass – 15-inch minimum size, creel limit 2 per day. Panfish – Creel limit 10 combined species per day. Use of live fish for bait is prohibited. Refer to [Big Bass Program](https://www.eregulations.com/pennsylvania/fishing/big-bass-program) for NO HARVEST dates.

  • All other trout species – 7 inches minimum size limit – 5 trout daily limit (combined species) from 8 a.m. on the opening day of the regular season for trout to midnight on Labor Day. Catch and release/no harvest – it is unlawful to take, kill or possess any trout from 12:01 a.m. the day after Labor Day to midnight the third Sunday in February the following year.

  • All species except trout – [Commonwealth Inland Waters](https://www.eregulations.com/pennsylvania/fishing/commonwealth-inland-waters) regulations apply. See § 61.1 (relating to Commonwealth inland waters[](https://www.eregulations.com/pennsylvania/fishing/commonwealth-inland-waters)).

Lake Pleasant
  • It is unlawful to use any fish, live or dead, as bait while fishing at Lake Pleasant except for “salted minnows.” Further, it is unlawful to release any fish into Lake Pleasant except for those caught while fishing.
Raystown Lake and Raystown Branch
  • – From dam downstream to Juniata River.
  • Trout (all species) no closed season. Creel limit 5 per day during regular season; 3 per day from the day after Labor Day to 8 a.m. on the opening day of the next regular season, minimum size – 7 inches.
  • _Raystown Lake and Tributaries_– Smelt may be taken with a dip net from the shore or by wading only. Dip net may not exceed 20 inches in diameter or square. Daily limit – 200 smelt or one gallon by volume, whichever is greater.

  • Open year-round to fishing. The daily limit is 3 trout (combined species) during the period from 8 a.m. on the opening day of trout season to midnight the third Monday in February the following year. Only 1 of the 3 trout daily limit may exceed 18 inches in length. No trout may be killed or had in possession from February 17 to the opening day of trout season. Inland regulations apply for other fish species.
