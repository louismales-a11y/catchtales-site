Pennsylvania offers exceptional fishing across 85,000 miles of streams and 4,000 lakes. Lake Erie provides world-class walleye and steelhead fishing, the Allegheny National Forest features remote trout streams, the Susquehanna River offers smallmouth bass fishing, and the Poconos provide outstanding trout fishing in classic mountain streams. The state has over 200 species of fish.
