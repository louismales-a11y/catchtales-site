Kentucky has 45 major lakes — over 100 man-made reservoirs and only 3 natural lakes. From massive Kentucky Lake and Lake Cumberland to the mountain lakes of the east, the Bluegrass State offers world-class bass, crappie, and muskie fishing.

Fishing Regulations

  • Fish not listed are rough fish - no size or creel limits unless special regulations apply.
  • Possession limit is 2x the daily creel limit.
  • Anglers may cull fish of a species until reaching the daily creel limit (except trout on Cumberland River).
  • Some waterbodies have different limits - check the Special Regulations section.
  • Lake sturgeon, alligator gar: catch and release only.
