Michigan has over 11,000 lakes larger than 5 acres — including 1,148 over 100 acres and 10 over 10,000 acres. Bordered by four Great Lakes (Michigan, Huron, Superior, and Erie), Michigan offers world-class fishing for trout, salmon, walleye, bass, pike, muskellunge, and panfish across six distinct regions.
