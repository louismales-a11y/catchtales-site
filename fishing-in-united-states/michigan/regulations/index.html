Michigan Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Inland waters = all waters except Great Lakes and connecting waters (St. Marys, St. Clair, Detroit rivers, Lake St. Clair).
  • ⚠ Great Lakes and inland waters have different rules - check the digest.
  • ⚠ Many waters have exceptions listed by county (Exceptions to General Regulations by County).
  • ⚠ Report marked/tagged fish through Eyes in the Field; do not remove tags from lake sturgeon.
  • ⚠ Great Lakes steelhead/trout often have adipose fin clips (coded-wire tags).
