North Dakota offers outstanding walleye fishing on massive Missouri River reservoirs and natural glacial lakes. Lake Sakakawea — the third-largest reservoir in the US at 368,000 acres — is famous for trophy walleye and northern pike. Devils Lake is one of the best walleye fisheries in North America, and the Missouri River below Garrison Dam provides excellent rainbow trout fishing in a unique tailwater setting.

Fishing Regulations

  • Daily creel limit = max legally taken fish (by species) midnight to midnight; possession = as listed.
  • Game fish and parts thereof may not be used as bait, except yellow perch eyes and trout/salmon eggs.
  • Channel catfish east of ND Hwy 1: may only remove gills/entrails on the water (head, fillets and tail attached).
  • Special dark-house spearing/bowfishing waters for pike, walleye, channel catfish (Devils Lake complex, Sakakawea, Oahe, Missouri/Yellowstone).
