Alaska Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Alaska regulations are area-based: the general limits below apply to Southeast Alaska/Yakutat; Southcentral, Interior, Arctic and Western areas have their own limits in the ADF&G regs book.
  • ⚠ Freshwater sport fishing season is open year-round except for king salmon closures between Cape Fairweather and Dixon Entrance.
  • ⚠ Check emergency orders before fishing (adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR) - many salmon limits are set in-season.
  • ⚠ Harvest records required: steelhead trout, sharks, and (for nonresidents) king salmon, lingcod, sablefish, demersal shelf rockfish.
  • ⚠ Alaska residents follow NONRESIDENT limits when fishing in the EEZ (3-200 nautical miles offshore).
  • ⚠ All saltwater vessels must carry a functional deepwater release device for rockfish; unreleased rockfish must be returned at depth or 100 ft.
  • ⚠ No person may possess filleted Pacific halibut on board (max 2 ventral + 2 dorsal + 2 cheek pieces with skin attached).
  • ⚠ Charter operators and crew may not keep king salmon or lingcod while clients are aboard.
  • ⚠ Motorized vehicles may not cross waters where salmon/trout/Dolly Varden/Arctic char/sheefish/whitefish spawn without a Fish Habitat permit.

Waterbody-Specific Rules

Kenai River (upper, middle, lower)
  • King salmon: annual limits differ by section (e.g., middle Kenai 1 king 20+ in for nonresidents, C&R in parts); check ADF&G regs + emergency orders.
Kasilof River
  • King salmon: 1-2/day per area regs; sockeye limits per area.
Russian River
  • Sockeye salmon: 3/day (Jun 11 - Aug 7); snagging restricted to specific periods; closed when escapement thresholds met.
Copper River (Glennallen area)
  • King salmon: 1/day, 20 in minimum; C&R in parts; sockeye per area.
Ship Creek (Anchorage)
  • King salmon: 2/day, 16 in minimum; snagging C&R periods.
Lake Louise
  • Rainbow trout: 2/day, 18 in minimum; lake trout 2/day.
Lake Iliamna
  • Sockeye salmon: 10/day (some waters); trout per area regs.
Yukon River
  • Chum salmon: 10/day; king salmon 2/day; seasonal per area.
Chena River (Fairbanks)
  • King salmon: 1/day, 20 in minimum (seasonal); C&R periods.
Chitina (Copper River personal-use)
  • Personal-use dipnet fishery - separate regulations (not sport).
Kenai River
  • King salmon: annual limits differ by section (upper/middle/lower); check ADF&G regs + emergency orders.

Seasons

Vary widely by area and species; salmon runs seasonal (typically May-September); some waters closed during spawning.

Licence Fees

Alaska sport fishing license (resident $29 annual; nonresident 1-day ~$25, annual ~$145); king salmon stamp for nonresidents; fees per ADF&G.
