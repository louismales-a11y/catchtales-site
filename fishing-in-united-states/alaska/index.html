Fishing Regulations

  • Alaska regulations are area-based: the general limits below apply to Southeast Alaska/Yakutat; Southcentral, Interior, Arctic and Western areas have their own limits in the ADF&G regs book.
  • Freshwater sport fishing season is open year-round except for king salmon closures between Cape Fairweather and Dixon Entrance.
  • Check emergency orders before fishing (adfg.alaska.gov/sf/EONR) - many salmon limits are set in-season.
  • Harvest records required: steelhead trout, sharks, and (for nonresidents) king salmon, lingcod, sablefish, demersal shelf rockfish.
  • Alaska residents follow NONRESIDENT limits when fishing in the EEZ (3-200 nautical miles offshore).
  • All saltwater vessels must carry a functional deepwater release device for rockfish; unreleased rockfish must be returned at depth or 100 ft.
  • No person may possess filleted Pacific halibut on board (max 2 ventral + 2 dorsal + 2 cheek pieces with skin attached).
  • Charter operators and crew may not keep king salmon or lingcod while clients are aboard.
  • Motorized vehicles may not cross waters where salmon/trout/Dolly Varden/Arctic char/sheefish/whitefish spawn without a Fish Habitat permit.
