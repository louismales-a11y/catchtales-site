Utah Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Limits below apply to most Utah fisheries; some waters have specific bag/size restrictions (see Rules for Specific Waters).
  • ⚠ All burbot caught must be immediately killed (do not release - invasive).
  • ⚠ Anglers may not possess kokanee salmon at any waterbody statewide from Sep 10 - Nov 30.
  • ⚠ Community fisheries: limit 2 fish of most species; release tiger muskellunge.
