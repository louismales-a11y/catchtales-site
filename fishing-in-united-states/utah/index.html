Utah offers exceptional fishing in dramatic settings from the Wasatch Front to the red rock country of the south. Bear Lake is famous for Bonneville cutthroat trout and cisco, Flaming Gorge Reservoir provides trophy lake trout and rainbow trout, Lake Powell offers bass and catfish in a stunning red rock canyon setting, and the Uinta Mountains feature hundreds of high-altitude trout lakes.
