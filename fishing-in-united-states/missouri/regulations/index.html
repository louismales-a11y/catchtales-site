Missouri Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Fishing methods: pole and line, trotline, throwline, limb line, bank line, jug line; ice fishing tackle/tip-ups are pole-and-line.
  • ⚠ Game fish not hooked in the mouth or jaw must be returned to the water unharmed immediately (except paddlefish taken legally by snagging).
  • ⚠ Possession limit is twice the statewide daily limit; catch must be labeled with name and address when away.
  • ⚠ Alligator gar are a protected species - it is illegal to take and possess them.
  • ⚠ Only the daily limit may be possessed on waters and banks of waters being fished.

Seasons

Most species open Jan 1 - Dec 31. Black bass in Ozark streams: May 23 - Feb 28. Paddlefish snagging: spring season; Mississippi River snagging Mar 15 - May 15 and Sep 15 - Dec 15.

Licence Fees

Missouri fishing permit required (resident/non-resident; trout permit for trout parks).
