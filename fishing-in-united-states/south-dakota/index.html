South Dakota features outstanding fishing on the Missouri River reservoirs and natural glacial lakes. Lake Oahe is world-famous for walleye, smallmouth bass, and Chinook salmon. The Black Hills offer excellent trout fishing in mountain streams and lakes, and the Glacial Lakes region in the northeast is a premier walleye and northern pike destination. The Missouri River below Fort Pierre provides exceptional walleye fishing.
