Maryland has zero natural lakes but over 100 man-made reservoirs, about 50 of which are stocked routinely. The Chesapeake Bay and Potomac River provide world-class striped bass (rockfish) and blue catfish fishing. Maryland also offers free fishing days and free fishing areas where no licence is needed.

Fishing Regulations

  • Bass catch-and-release permitted at all times; bass cannot be used as bait.
  • Herring (alewife/blueback) and shad: CLOSED - catch and release only.
  • Atlantic/shortnose sturgeon: closed.
  • Bass slot areas (Johnson Pond, Leonard Mill Pond, Little Seneca Lake, St. Mary's Lake): no bass 11-15 in, only 1 over 15 in, 5/day.
  • Catch-and-release bass areas: Monocacy River, Patapsco River (I-70 to MD 144), Potomac River (Dam 2 to Monocacy mouth).
