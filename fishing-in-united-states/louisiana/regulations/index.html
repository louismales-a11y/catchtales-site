Louisiana Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Possession limit is TWICE the daily creel limit unless otherwise stated.
  • ⚠ Sturgeon: no legal harvest or possession.
  • ⚠ Paddlefish cannot be retained alive and cannot be harvested by snagging; 30-in max lower-jaw fork length.
  • ⚠ Anglers may have up to 3x daily bag limit of black bass below US 90 in coastal Louisiana if fish kept whole in separate labeled bags.

Waterbody-Specific Rules

Lower Atchafalaya Basin, Grassy Lake, Lake Palourde, Lake Verrett
  • Black bass 12 in MLL, 5 daily (effective 8/1/2026).
Bussey Brake
  • Bass 5/day, 18 in max length (1 over 22 in for weigh-in); crappie 10 in min, 25/day; sunfish 50.
Eagle Lake (Madison Parish)
  • Largemouth bass min 16 in; crappie 11 in min, 30/day.
Toledo Bend & Sabine River
  • Bass 14 in MLL (8 daily); crappie 25 daily; catfish 50 aggregate.
Caddo Lake
  • Crappie 25/day; white bass 25; catfish 50 aggregate (blue/channel no size, flathead 18 in/10 daily).
Toledo Bend Reservoir
  • Black bass 14 in minimum, 8/day; crappie 25/day; white bass 25.
Sabine River
  • Black bass 14 in minimum (above Toledo Bend), 8/day; crappie 25.
D'Arbonne Lake
  • Crappie: 50/day, max 7 over 12 in.
Lake Verret
  • Black bass 12 in min, 5/day (Atchafalaya area).
Lower Atchafalaya Basin
  • Black bass 12 in min, 5/day; crappie 8 in min, 25/day.

Seasons

Most species open year-round; paddlefish restricted.

Licence Fees

Louisiana fishing license required (resident/non-resident); fees per LDWF.
