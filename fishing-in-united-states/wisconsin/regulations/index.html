Wisconsin Fishing Regulations

Not legal advice. Compiled from official government summaries. Regulations change each year — always check the official source before relying.

General Rules (barbless hooks are the #1 cause of fines)

  • ⚠ Total daily bag limit = max fish from all waters in one day; daily bag limit per waterbody also applies.
  • ⚠ Opening day is traditionally the first Saturday in May.
  • ⚠ Wisconsin has ~2000 special-regulation lakes - ALWAYS check the Special Regulations listed by county for the specific water.
  • ⚠ Catch-and-release for bass is allowed at all other times of the year.
  • ⚠ Species not listed have no open season (e.g., American eel, grass pickerel).
  • ⚠ Paddlefish: no open season in Wisconsin.

Seasons

General inland fishing May 3 - Mar 1; musky May 24 - Dec 31 (north); trout seasons per stream classification; bass C&R outside harvest season.

Licence Fees

Wisconsin fishing license required (resident/non-resident); Great Lakes salmon/trout stamp; trout stamp for trout waters; sturgeon spearing license for Winnebago system.
