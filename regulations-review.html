Saskatchewan Anglers' Guide 2026-27

Effective: May 2026 - Apr 2027 · Source: publications.saskatchewan.ca
⚠ HOOK RULES — Saskatchewan Required on catch-and-release waters
  • Barbless hooks are required on all catch-and-release (CR) waters, plus Lake of the Prairies, Athapapuskow and Tobin lakes.
  • Check each waterbody's status in the special regulations (pages 10-27).

General limits (provincial Crown waters)

Catch limits
· 
Limit refers to the maximum number and size of fish you are allowed to retain or 
have in your possession. Anglers may not possess more than the limit of each 
fish species for both size and number of fish. This includes fish that are eaten or 
given away that day, as well as all fish that are at your camp, being transported 
by or for you, or fish that are in storage. 
· 
General limits apply to most provincial Crown waters in Saskatchewan. 
However, some waters have special regulations that further restrict angler 
harvest or activity. These exceptions to the general regulations must be 
complied with when fishing any of the listed waters (see page 10-27).

British Columbia Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis

Effective: Apr 1, 2025 - Mar 31, 2027 (current 2-yr ed.) · Source: gov.bc.ca + DFO Pacific (8 regions)
⚠ HOOK RULES — British Columbia MANDATORY IN ALL RIVERS
  • Barbed hooks are ILLEGAL in any river, stream, creek or slough in BC (barbless mandatory there all year).
  • Barbed hooks are permitted in lakes unless the water-specific table says otherwise.
  • Region 1 streams: single barbless hook, all year.

Region quotas (e.g. Region 1 - Vancouver Island)

Trout: 4 (max 1 over 50 cm; max 2 from streams, hatchery only) · Bass: unlimited · Kokanee: 5 · White Sturgeon: catch & release · Crayfish: 25. Quotas differ per region.

Licences: Tidal & freshwater licence fees vary; annual BC freshwater licence $36 (resident 16-65) per gov.bc.ca.

Salmon rules (DFO): daily limit all Pacific salmon 4 combined, possession twice daily, max 8; per-region openings/closures on pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca.
