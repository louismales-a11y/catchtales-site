New Brunswick has over 2,500 lakes and countless rivers flowing into the Bay of Fundy and Gulf of St. Lawrence. The Miramichi River system is one of the world's most famous Atlantic salmon destinations, while the Saint John River offers excellent smallmouth bass, striped bass, and muskie fishing. Grand Lake and the Chiputneticook Lakes provide outstanding bass and chain pickerel fishing, and the Bay of Fundy's record tides create unique tidal fishing opportunities for pollock, cod, and haddock.
