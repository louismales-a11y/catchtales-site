Newfoundland and Labrador offer some of North America's most remote and pristine fishing across a vast subarctic landscape. The island's Atlantic salmon rivers - the Humber, Gander, and Exploits - are world-renowned, while countless ponds and lakes produce trophy brook trout exceeding five pounds.
